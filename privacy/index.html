Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ScoreFlow handles information when you use the ScoreFlow mobile application.

Information You Provide

ScoreFlow allows you to import or scan sheet music for use within the application. Files or images you choose to process may be temporarily transmitted to services used to convert musical notation into a format that ScoreFlow can display and play.

You control which files or images you choose to import or scan.

Camera Access

ScoreFlow may request access to your device camera so you can capture sheet music for processing. Camera access is only used when you choose to use a feature that requires it.

Local Storage

Your ScoreFlow library and processed score files are stored locally on your device.

ScoreFlow currently does not provide cloud backup or synchronization of your personal score library. Deleting the application may also delete locally stored scores and application data.

Subscriptions and Purchases

ScoreFlow offers optional paid subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed by Apple. ScoreFlow does not receive or store your payment card information.

ScoreFlow uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status and determine whether ScoreFlow Pro features are available to you. RevenueCat may process purchase-related identifiers and subscription information necessary to provide these services.

Third-Party Services

ScoreFlow may use third-party services necessary to operate features such as subscription management and processing of imported or scanned sheet music.

These service providers may process limited information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy policies.

Personal Accounts

ScoreFlow currently does not require you to create a ScoreFlow account to use the application.

Data We Do Not Intentionally Collect

ScoreFlow does not intentionally collect your contacts, precise location, health information, or payment card details.

Children's Privacy

ScoreFlow is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ScoreFlow evolves. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
