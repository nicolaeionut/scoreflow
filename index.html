Scanning & Importing Scores

ScoreFlow lets you scan or import sheet music and turn it into an interactive piano practice experience.

If a score cannot be processed, make sure the image or document is clear and that the musical notation is easy to read.

ScoreFlow Pro

ScoreFlow Pro provides unlimited scans and unlimited saved scores. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased ScoreFlow Pro and it is not recognized, open the upgrade screen and select Restore Purchases. Make sure you are using the same Apple Account that was used for the original purchase.

Manage or Cancel a Subscription

Subscriptions can be managed from your iPhone under Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
